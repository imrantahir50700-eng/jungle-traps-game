HOW TO PLAY

🏃
Reach the golden door. Run with ◀ ▶ (or A / D / arrow keys), jump with ⬆ (or W / Space). Short hop = tap, long jump = hold.
⚠
The temple lies. Floors fall, spikes hide, doors run away. Every death teaches you one trap. Respawn is instant — death is just information.
🌀
The temple shifts its traps. The temple shifts its traps after every fall — never trust a memorised path. The main hurdle moves: by the door, in the middle, near the start…
🚩
Checkpoints save your place. Touch a tribal flag to light it. After that, you respawn at the flag instead of the start.
◆
10 crystal keys are hidden. Find 4 to open the Crystal Caves. Find all 10 and the hero earns the golden hat.
★
Stars reward courage. Finish a level under its death target for 3 stars. Dying a lot? A hint appears — and eventually a skip. No shame, the temple wins sometimes.
🎭
Watch for new tricks: poison darts, bombs, levers (some lie), spinning stone saws, steam geysers and your own shadow.
